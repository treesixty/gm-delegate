# Setting

> Catalog file. Stable reference. Fill with your campaign; the agent reads this to ground
> tone and stakes. The example below shows the shape, replace it.

## Tone

Grounded low-fantasy. Danger is real, magic is uncommon, consequences stick.

## Large story goals (the shape of the campaign, not the secrets)

- The northern trade road has been closing. Nobody agrees why.
- The party is nominally working for the Thornwood wardens.

## What the agent may lean on

The public, player-known facts above. Anything hidden, the *reason* the road is closing,
who benefits, lives in `never-delegate.md`, not here.
