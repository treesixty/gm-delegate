# <Location name>

> Catalog file. One per location. The watch stage can link here; the scene stage reads it
> for ambient beats.

## What it is

One line. The kind of place, its function.

## Sensory anchors

3-4 fragments the GM performs from: a sound, a smell, the light, one telling detail.
Not a paragraph.

## Who/what is here

Standing NPCs (link to `_npcs/`), typical activity, anything a party would notice.

## GM-only

Hidden features, secrets, what a search turns up. Read for consistency, never revealed
unprompted.
