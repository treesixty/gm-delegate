## What it is

The northern trade road where it threads through old forest. Travellers thinning out.

## Sensory anchors

- Wet bark and cold iron smell after rain.
- Canopy so thick the light goes green.
- Cart ruts, but fresh ones are rare now.
- Birdsong stops before you notice it stopped.
