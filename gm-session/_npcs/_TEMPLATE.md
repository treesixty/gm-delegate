# <NPC name>

> Catalog file. One per significant NPC. The watch stage links to this when a moment is
> about this NPC; the scene stage reads it for voice and disposition.

## Public

Who the players know this person as. Role, manner, first impression.

## Voice

2-3 cues the GM performs from: cadence, a verbal tic, what they do with their hands.
Fragments, not a monologue.

## Disposition (current)

How they feel about the party right now. Update between sessions.

## GM-only

Secrets, leverage, what they actually want. The agent reads this to stay consistent but
NEVER voices it or reveals it. If a beat would expose it, that beat escalates.

## Delegable?

`yes` / `no`. If no, the NPC-voice stage never proposes for this actor regardless of
policy. Villains and quest-critical NPCs are `no`.
