# Halda, the Thornwood innkeeper

> Example NPC. Replace or delete. Shows the shape a linked NPC file takes.

## Public

Runs the Kettle & Bough. Brisk, unsentimental, knows everyone on the road.

## Voice

- Clipped sentences. Never wastes a word.
- Wipes the same spot on the bar when thinking.
- Calls everyone "traveller" until they've earned a name.

## Disposition (current)

Wary but not hostile. The party paid their tab; that buys civility, not trust.

## GM-only

Her supplier stopped coming three weeks ago. She's frightened, hiding it as annoyance.
Do not voice the fear. It escalates.

## Delegable?

yes
