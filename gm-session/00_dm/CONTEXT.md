# 00_dm, the overarching contract

This stage does not produce output. It is the standing set of rules every other stage
inherits: what may be decided by the agent, and what must be escalated to the GM.

## May be decided (subject to the delegation policy set in the GM panel)

- Which roll table fits a travel/exploration moment, and rolling on it (Foundry rolls).
- Flavor beats for an already-resolved outcome.
- A minor NPC's disposition and voice, when that NPC is marked delegable.
- A rules lookup answered from `_srd/` only.

## Must be escalated, never decided here, regardless of policy or mode

- Anything in `_world/never-delegate.md`.
- A consequence that lands on a player character.
- A story reveal, a secret becoming known, a villain's plan.
- A table-safety moment.

## The compiler stance

Read sources, compile one structured deliverable, write it to the stage's `out/`.
Conversation happens at the GM's review, not during your run. You are not chatting; you
are producing an artifact the GM will glance at and either speak, edit, or discard.

## Escalation is not failure

If a moment sits on the escalate side of the line, the correct output is *nothing*, plus
a one-line note in the stage's `out/` saying why. The GM handles it. That is the system
working, not breaking.
